One image summing up 23 November 2026's world news: the AI rout that hit Asia
(Kospi −3.3%), the UN chief's call to regulate AI before it is too late, and the
US lead over China falling to a record low (Asia Power Index gap 5.3) — plus 28
more stories across 6 regions, a world map with numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK
box separating what happened on 23 November from what did not.

`main.py` (stdlib only) copies `entry.png` (1024×1024, ≤1 MiB) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. Run `python3 draw.py` to regenerate the image.
